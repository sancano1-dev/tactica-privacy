Tactica Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

Tactica: Chess Practice ("Tactica", Android package com.tactica.chess) is provided by PSC Group. Contact: sancano1@hotmail.com.

Summary

Tactica does not require an account and does not collect, sell, or share personal data. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or payment SDKs and has no developer-operated server.

Data stored on your device

To let you resume play, Tactica stores the current game, move history, game mode, chosen side, computer level, and elapsed clocks on your device. Puzzle training stores XP, puzzle rating, solved puzzles, streak dates, and daily-puzzle completion on your device. This data never leaves your device through the app. It is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling Tactica deletes it.

Computer play and puzzles

Computer moves are calculated on your device. The puzzle collection, fonts, and lessons are bundled with the app, so core features work offline.

Device features

Tactica may use short vibrations (haptic feedback). It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

External links

Optional reference links open third-party websites outside the app. Those sites may receive standard network and browser information and are governed by their own privacy policies. Tactica does not add your game data to these links.

Support requests

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. We use it only to reply to you and delete it once your request is resolved, unless we must keep it to meet a legal obligation. You can ask us to delete your correspondence at any time.

Children

Tactica does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If our practices change, we will update this page and its effective date.
